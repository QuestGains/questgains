Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

QuestGains ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the QuestGains app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account and use the app:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Fitness Data: Workout logs, exercise records, body weight entries, nutrition logs, and progress data you enter
  • App Usage Data: XP earned, quests completed, streaks, level, and hero class selection

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your fitness data across devices
  • To calculate XP, track streaks, and power app features
  • To send optional push notifications (only with your permission)

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@questgains.app.

Children's Privacy

QuestGains is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@questgains.app
