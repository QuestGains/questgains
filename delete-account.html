How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the QuestGains app:

  1. Open the QuestGains app
  2. Tap your Hero tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll down to Settings
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion — your account will be permanently removed

Can't Access the App?

Email us directly and we will delete your account within 30 days:

support@questgains.com

⚠️ What gets deleted: Your account, username, hero name, all workout logs, cardio logs, XP history, badges, friends list, and all associated data stored in our systems.

What is retained: We do not retain any personal data after account deletion. Anonymized aggregate statistics (not linked to you) may be retained for app performance analysis.
